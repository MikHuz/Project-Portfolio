## Company Overview

**Doorgi Garage Doors** is a locally owned and operated garage door service company based in the San Francisco Bay Area.  

Established in 2023, we specialize in honest, transparent residential garage door **repair, installation, maintenance, and replacement**. Our entire business is built on one core belief: homeowners deserve straightforward, expert care without pressure, hidden fees, or unnecessary upsells.

- **Main Headquarters**: 1659 Scott Blvd Ste 160, Santa Clara, CA 95050  
- **Phone**: (408) 256-2727  
- **Email**: help@doorgi.com  
- **Website**: https://doorgi.com/

We proudly serve the entire Greater Bay Area and surrounding Northern California counties (see full service area below).

## Core Brand Values

At Doorgi, every decision is guided by these non-negotiable values:

- **Honesty & Transparency** — Clear explanations and upfront pricing, no surprises.
- **Doing the Job Right** — Obsessed with quality, detail, and workmanship that lasts.
- **Customer Education** — We explain issues and options so homeowners can make informed decisions.
- **Fairness & Respect** — No overcharging, no hidden fees, no pushing unnecessary services.
- **Safety & Reliability** — Every job prioritizes safety and long-term performance.

**Brand Promise**: We always put the customer’s needs first and deliver honest, reliable solutions with clear guidance and transparent pricing.

## Services Overview

We handle everything from minor tune-ups to full emergencies:

- Broken springs (always replace both)
- Broken motor / opener replacement
- Door off track
- Loose cables
- Damaged panels
- Bent tracks
- Panel gaps
- Noisy doors
- Failed rollers & cracked hinges
- Full maintenance & tune-ups
- Professional garage door installation (C.H.I. Elite Dealer)
- Emergency service

**Garage Door Openers**: We specialize in LiftMaster installations and strongly recommend their reliable, quiet, smart models (98022 Elite, 6690L Premium, 2420L Essential).

**Free Estimates**: Always free, no strings attached. If we don’t do the work, you pay nothing.
