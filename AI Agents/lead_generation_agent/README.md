# Lead Generation Agent – README

## Overview

The **Lead Generation Agent** is an automated AI system designed to proactively identify and capture new business opportunities from online sources. It was built to expand the reach of the service business by continuously monitoring popular platforms where potential customers post requests for services.

## Core Functionality

The agent actively scans and processes leads from multiple sources, such as :

- **Facebook** and **Facebook Marketplace**
- **Nextdoor**
- **Thumbtack**
- Other relevant business-oriented platforms and local community groups

### Lead Handling Workflow

1. **Discovery**: Monitors selected platforms for new posts matching the business’s service categories.
2. **Evaluation**: Assesses the relevance and quality of potential leads based on predefined criteria.
3. **Initial Response**: Crafts appropriate, professional outreach messages when suitable.
4. **Offsourcing**: Forwards promising leads to the business team for final review and approval.
5. **Tracking & Management**: Logs all identified leads in a structured format for follow-up, CRM integration, and performance analysis.

This workflow allowed the business to receive a steady stream of new potential customers with minimal manual searching effort.

## Key Features

- **Automated Platform Monitoring**: Scans public posts and marketplace listings in relevant categories.
- **Context-Aware Responses**: Generates personalized initial messages that align with the company’s brand voice.
- **Lead Qualification**: Filters out low-quality or irrelevant opportunities to reduce noise.
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: Sends promising leads to designated business users for review before deeper engagement.
- **Lead Tracking**: Maintains records of sourced leads, response status, and outcomes for reporting and optimization.

## Benefits

- Increased volume of inbound leads without proportional increase in manual effort.
- Faster response time to new customer requests compared to manual monitoring.
- Better organization and tracking of lead sources and conversion rates.
- Scalable way to grow the customer base through digital channels.

## Technical Approach

- Built with careful prompt engineering for reliable information extraction and response generation.
- Designed to respect platform terms of service and ethical lead generation practices.
- Integrated with internal tools for seamless handoff to the sales/operations team.
